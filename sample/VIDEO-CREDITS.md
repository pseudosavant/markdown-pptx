# Big Buck Bunny sample video

The clip in `big-buck-bunny-clip.mp4` contains the first 30 seconds of *Big Buck Bunny* at 854 by 480 pixels. It uses H.264 video and AAC audio. The poster in `big-buck-bunny-poster.png` is a frame from the same clip.
